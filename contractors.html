Important — How LeadForge Works

LeadForge is a platform that connects homeowners with contractors they choose.

Contractors do not pay to join the platform. Instead, when a homeowner submits a request and you are the selected contractor, you may be given the option to unlock full project details.

Unlocking a request is a one-time access fee that allows you to view the homeowner’s full information and pursue the opportunity.

LeadForge does not guarantee that any unlocked request will result in a job, contract, or payment. Contractors are responsible for their own pricing, agreements, and project outcomes.
